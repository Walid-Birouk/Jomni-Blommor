## Platform

web

## Users

Local people in Stockholm, mostly on phones, often arriving from Google Maps or Instagram. Their jobs, in order of frequency (assumed, confirm with client):

- Order or pick up a bouquet for a birthday, thank-you or no reason.
- Arrange flowers for a wedding or a funeral, often under time pressure or grief.
- Book flowers for an event or a business.
- Find the shop: where it is and whether it is open now.

Confirmed by the developer on 10 Nov 2026: this audience is right.

## Product Purpose

A small-business website for Jomni Blommor, a florist on Södermalm in Stockholm. In priority order, the site must:

1. Get people to contact or visit the shop (call, message, order, book, find).
2. Show the work. Photos of bouquets and arrangements are the product.
3. Be found locally for searches like "blomsterbutik Södermalm", "florist nära mig", "begravningsblommor Stockholm".

Success: more calls, orders and visits, and the site ranking next to the Google Business Profile for local florist searches. The listing currently has no website linked.

## Positioning

**Open decision.** What sets Jomni Blommor apart from nearby florists is not yet known and must come from the client. Do not invent a speciality, style or origin story.

Context only, not for publication: Google lists nearby alternatives with ratings between 3.2 and 5.0, including The Mayflower, Kaprifol Blomsterhandel, blomsterbana blommor, Södermalms Blommor & Växter and Blomsterpassagen Hornstull.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive on phones, often standing on the street or comparing shops in Google Maps.
- Peak demand days: Alla hjärtans dag, Mors dag, studenten, midsommar, Allhelgona, jul.
- Funeral orders come from people in grief, so tone there must be calm and clear.
- Swedish is the primary language and English is the secondary one.

## Product Principles

1. Getting in touch beats everything. Every page ends with a way to call, order or find the shop.
2. The flowers sell themselves. Real photos lead, and the interface stays out of the way.
3. Only true facts. Unknowns stay as visible placeholders until the client confirms them.
4. Local first. Address, opening status and directions are always one tap away on a phone.
